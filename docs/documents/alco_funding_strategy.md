# ALCO Funding and Hedging Strategy: Q4 2026

Document owner: Group Treasurer. Classification: Confidential - Treasury only.

## Context

The Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO) met on 14 October 2026 to review the balance sheet position at 30 September 2026. The committee reviewed IRRBB results, deposit behaviour, liquidity buffers and the funding plan for the fourth quarter.

The main concern was EVE sensitivity to rising rates. The largest EVE decline was 11.1 percent of Tier 1 capital under the parallel up scenario, close to the internal early-warning limit of 12 percent. Deposit balances had also shifted from current accounts towards instant-access savings, which have a higher repricing beta.

## Hedging decisions

ALCO approved an increase of $1.5 billion notional in pay-fixed interest rate swaps with a 5-year tenor, executed in three tranches before 15 December 2026. The hedge is expected to reduce the parallel up EVE decline from 11.1 percent to about 8.9 percent of Tier 1 capital.

The committee rejected a proposal to extend the duration of the investment portfolio, because it would increase EVE sensitivity and use more of the high-quality assets in the liquidity buffer.

Hedge effectiveness will be monitored monthly by Treasury and reported to ALCO with the regular IRRBB results.

## Deposit pricing

ALCO decided to hold the instant-access savings rate unchanged for the quarter. Treasury estimated that a 25 basis point increase would retain about $300 million of balances at an annual cost of $28 million, which the committee judged too expensive.

Treasury will track weekly savings outflows. If outflows exceed $500 million in any four-week period, the pricing decision will be reviewed at an extraordinary ALCO meeting.

## Liquidity and funding

The liquidity coverage ratio stood at 142 percent at 30 September 2026. ALCO set a management target of at least 135 percent, above the regulatory minimum of 100 percent. The committee approved a $750 million senior unsecured issuance in November to pre-fund maturities in the first quarter of 2027.

## Dependencies and data

These decisions rely on the IRRBB engine results, the non-maturity deposit model and the daily liquidity figures. ALCO noted that the stale IRRBB run on 30 October 2026, caused by a late general ledger snapshot, had not affected any decision because the committee uses month-end figures.

This strategy is confidential. It must not be shared outside Treasury before the hedges are fully executed.
